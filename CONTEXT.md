# Virtual wardrobe

A person keeps track of clothes and accessories and chooses what to wear.

## Language

**Wardrobe**
One person’s private collection of recorded clothes and accessories.

**Wardrobe item**
An individual piece of clothing or an accessory in the wardrobe.

**Detected purchase**
A piece identified in an order confirmation. It becomes a wardrobe item when the owner confirms they kept it.

**Outfit**
A set of pieces chosen to be worn together.
